# Planetary Texture Credits

All planetary equirectangular maps in this directory are derived from real NASA/JPL/USGS spacecraft observations and are redistributed under compatible open licenses:

- **Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth (+ Clouds), Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn (+ Rings), Uranus, Neptune**:
  - Source: [Solar System Scope Textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) (based on NASA elevation and spacecraft imagery data)
  - License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))
  - Modifications: Resampled to 2048×1024 JPEG; Earth clouds composited softly over the Earth day map; Saturn ring alpha resampled to a 256×1 radial profile.
- **Io**:
  - Source: NASA / JPL / USGS Galileo & Voyager global mosaic (`Io from Galileo and Voyager missions.jpg`, Wikimedia Commons)
  - License: Public Domain (`PD-USGov-NASA`)
- **Europa**:
  - Source: Voyager & Galileo SSI global color map (`Moon Europa color map.jpg`, Wikimedia Commons)
